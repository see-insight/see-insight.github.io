# SEE-Insight Team Charter

The following is our team code of conduct and rules all team members should abide by.

Remember:

### **S**trive to be better than yesterday

### **E**mpower others

### **E**xceed your limits

**Be Respectful:** Be kind to others, wait for your turn when speaking, and be respectful to different viewpoints you may not have thought of before.

**Listen Actively:** Pay attention to others when they're speaking, offer constructive feedback, and be open to ideas from anyone.

**Take Initiative:** Be proactive in solving problems. Try to figure out problems yourself first, then seek help from others. Don't be afraid to speak up about obstacles that may be in our way.

**Take Responsibility:** Do your best work, own up to your mistakes, and learn to improve yourself for the future.

**Have Integrity:** Do your own work, give others credit where credit is due, and be honest about problems you may have.

**Be a Leader:** Communicate with your teammates, help divide work fairly when appropriate, and step outside your comfort zone to support others when needed.

**Be Punctual:** Show up to team meetings on time, respect the schedules of others, and finish your assigned work by the given deadline.

## **Communication Rules**

### 1. **Microsoft Teams**
   - Use the general channel for updates everyone should see.
   - Use the random channel for off-topic updates.
   - Use the appropriate project channel in hidden channels to talk about specific projects.
   - Share project files to your specific project's shared tab.
   - Respond to requests in a timely manner.
### 2. **Email**
   - Use email for urgent and important messages.
   - Write up emails so that they are complete, structured, and grammatically correct.
### 3. **GitHub**
   - Use GitHub comments to provide updated status or instructions for a task.
   - Assign yourself to the main tasks you are working on.
   - Move tasks to the correct status column.
   - See project repository guidelines [here](https://colbrydi.github.io/Research_guidelines/Rules_for_Repos.html).
